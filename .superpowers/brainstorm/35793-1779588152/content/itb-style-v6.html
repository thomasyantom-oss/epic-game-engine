更新后的完整原语列表

原语 类别 参数
pulse 发起 actor, color
flash_sequence 发起 path[], color
projectile 发起 actor, target, shape(circle/diamond/crescent), trail, speed, color, size
beam 发起 actor, target, color, width
slash 发起 target(s), color, arc(small/large), direction
impact 命中 target, color(default: white)
shake 命中 target, intensity(light/normal/heavy)
damage_number 命中 target, value, color(damage/heal/miss)
buff_up 状态 target/field, color, intensity(normal/big)
debuff_down 状态 target/field, color, intensity(normal/big)
mark_dead 结果 target
indicator_add 结果 target, corner, color
